## Notes

- **Dromina National School, Co. Cork - yard time** - Real children at break. The school's memory.
- **VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition** - Upright pianos, string sections, bowed cymbal, gong scrape, drum-head friction, brick scrape, chain grind, metal hits, organ blower.
- **Versilian Community Sample Library** - Upright piano with release samples, bowed brake drum, bowed vibraphone, wine glasses, bowed psaltery.
- **Kokoro-82M TTS (ONNX export)** - Neural TTS used as raw material for every authored line; all output is resynthesised (WORLD) before use.
- **LibriSpeech dev-clean (read English speech, 40 speakers)** - Real human voices for the far-away crowd/assembly babble; never intelligible in the output.
- **MIT KEMAR HRTF, normal pinna (SOFA)** - Binaural rendering of every positioned source. Gardner, W. G., and Martin, K. D. (1995). HRTF measurements of a KEMAR. JASA 97, 3907-3908.
- **Aachen Impulse Response database 1.4** - Real binaural (dummy-head) room responses: stairway, lecture room, Aula Carolina hall.
